# Verified Research Papers

This collection contains verified scholarly research papers related to designing verification checkpoints to mitigate silent errors in Agentic AI pipelines.

The papers are organized by research theme. Each paper is selected for its relevance to agentic AI, large language model reliability, verification, evaluation, monitoring, safety, or failure detection.

## 1. Survey and Review Papers

Research surveys and reviews covering the foundations, capabilities, risks, and evaluation of large language models and AI agents.

## 2. Foundational Research

Foundational papers on large language models, reasoning, tool use, and autonomous AI systems.

## 3. Verification and Self-Checking

Research on methods for checking, verifying, critiquing, and correcting AI-generated outputs.

## 4. Agentic AI Reliability and Evaluation

Research focused on evaluating AI agents, measuring reliability, detecting failures, and understanding agent behavior.

## 5. Tool-Using and Multi-Agent Systems

Research on AI systems that use external tools, interact with environments, or collaborate through multiple agents.

## 6. Safety, Monitoring and Failure Detection

Research related to AI safety, monitoring, guardrails, error detection, and preventing harmful or silent failures.
