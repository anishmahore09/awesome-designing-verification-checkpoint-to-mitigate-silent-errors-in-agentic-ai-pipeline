# Citation Integrity Audit

## Purpose

This audit documents the verification of references used in the AI-assisted research paper, *Designing Verification Checkpoints to Mitigate Silent Errors in Agentic AI Pipelines*. The purpose is to ensure that cited scholarly works genuinely exist and that their bibliographic information and links correspond to the referenced papers.

## Verification Criteria

Each selected reference was checked for:

- Correct paper title
- Correct authors
- Publication year
- Journal or conference, where applicable
- Availability of a DOI, publisher page, or authoritative paper link
- Existence of the cited paper
- Relevance of the paper to the research topic
- Correspondence between the paper and the provided link

## Verification Sources

The references were checked using authoritative scholarly sources and research repositories, including:

- Google Scholar
- arXiv
- ACM Digital Library
- NeurIPS proceedings
- ICLR proceedings
- ACL Anthology
- Nature
- Official project or publisher pages

## Verification Summary

The research collection was reviewed to remove unsupported or fabricated references and to ensure that the listed papers correspond to real scholarly publications or established research works. Links were selected from publisher pages, official conference proceedings, arXiv, or recognized project pages where available.

The collection focuses on research related to LLM reliability, verification, reasoning, hallucination detection, agent evaluation, tool use, monitoring, and AI safety.

## Important Note

Generative AI tools may assist with discovering candidate references, but references were not accepted solely because they were suggested by an AI system. Bibliographic details and source links were independently checked before inclusion in the repository.
